Embedding map

Each dot is one person's stored 128-number profile, flattened to two dimensions. Grey dots are held-out Aalto people; coloured dots are people enrolled here.

Computing layout… the first load after the backend starts can take up to 30 seconds while UMAP compiles.
Scroll to zoom · drag to pan · click a dot to pin it

The axes have no units, and distances on the map are approximate: roughly half of a dot's true nearest neighbours stay nearby after flattening 128 dimensions to 2. Trust the “Nearest in 128-D” list, which is exact. Lines join the hovered dot to those neighbours.
